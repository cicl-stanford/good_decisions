Expected benefit of acting

Enter experimental and observational data, and the reward (or cost) of acting on each counterfactual type. The calculator combines both data sources to bound the proportion of each type, using the Tian–Pearl bounds as presented in Mueller & Pearl (2022), and returns the expected benefit of handing out the flyer (acting) rather than not.

without flyer P(y | do(x'))

Chance of getting a ticket when the flyer is randomly handed out or withheld.

Observational data (flyer obtainable)

P(ticket)
with flyer P(y | x)
without flyer P(y | x')

Chance of getting a ticket among people who chose to take the flyer, and among those who didn't. Half of the people are assumed to take the flyer, P(x) = 0.5, as in the experiments.
